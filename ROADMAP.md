# NR3DS roadmap

## Stable baseline through v0.010

- Citro3D 3D renderer
- Old 3DS-oriented native vehicle physics
- High-speed precision steering
- Wall sliding / traffic collision handling
- Curved highway and traffic AI
- Rival sprint race with checkpoints and results
- Cash/reward loop
- Engine, turbo and tire upgrades
- Final-drive and per-gear tuning
- Japanese tuning-shop/showroom visual pass
- More detailed low-poly display car
- Cleaner garage HUD

## Next: v0.011 - Midnight expressway overhaul

- Narrower Japanese urban-expressway proportions
- Better lane/barrier spacing
- Tunnels and tunnel-light sequences
- Overhead route signs and gantries
- More varied roadside structures
- Denser but readable night traffic
- Stronger speed sensation and road lighting
- Improved chase-camera presentation
- Keep v0.010 physics/steering stable

## v0.012 - Highway rival battles

- Rolling rival encounters
- Challenge/duel transition
- Gap-based highway battle rules
- Rival personalities and route behavior
- More organic opponent placement than the current checkpoint sprint

## Later milestones

- Persistent save data
- Better low-poly car meshes and texture atlases
- Larger streamed road network / route splits
- Additional shops and progression
- Audio / engine note / turbo / tire sounds
- More faithful recovered handling curves
- Performance validation on a physical Old 3DS
